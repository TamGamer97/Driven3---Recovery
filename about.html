Our story

Built on the bad nights.

In 2016, Adam Rahman broke down on the M62 at Windy Hill with his newborn daughter in the back. He waited three hours for a recovery truck that had been sent from Sheffield. By the time it arrived, he and his brothers had already decided they could do it better.

They bought a second-hand Iveco with their savings, painted it themselves and parked it on their mum's drive in Chadderton. Yusuf took the nights, Bilal took the days, and Adam answered the phone — every single call, for two years.

We've grown since then. But we've kept the bits that mattered: drivers who live where they work, prices we stand behind, and treating every call like it's someone in our own family stood on that hard shoulder.
