How it works

  1. Give us the registration, online or over the phone.
  2. We make you an offer. You don’t have to accept it.
  3. We collect the car free of charge at a time that suits you.
  4. You’re paid on collection and we notify the DVLA.

What we take

  • Cars that have failed their MOT
  • Non-runners
  • Accident-damaged cars
  • Old and unwanted cars and vans

Paperwork

Vehicles go to an Authorised Treatment Facility. You’ll get a Certificate of Destruction and the DVLA will be notified. Please have your V5C logbook and photo ID ready when we collect.

Payment is made by bank transfer, as required by the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013.
