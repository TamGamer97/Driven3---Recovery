Who pays?

For eligible non-fault claims, the cost of recovery, storage and a replacement vehicle may be recovered from the at-fault driver’s insurer. That means you don’t have to pay upfront.

What to do after an accident

  1. Make sure everyone is safe. Call 999 if anyone is hurt or the road is blocked.
  2. Swap names, addresses, registration numbers and insurance details with the other driver.
  3. Take photos of the vehicles, the damage and the scene.
  4. Call us on 07809 191292.
