FAQ

Quick answers.

Stuck right now?

Skip the reading. Call us and we'll talk you through it while the truck's on its way.

How much will it cost?

Most local recoveries are £75–£120. We give you a fixed price on the phone before we set off, and that's what you pay. You can also estimate it yourself.

Do I need to be a member?

No. You pay for the job you need, when you need it. No subscription, no joining fee.

I'm on the motorway. What should I do while I wait?

Pull onto the hard shoulder or an emergency area, put your hazards on, and get everyone out on the passenger side and behind the barrier. Don't try to fix anything yourself.

If you can't get out safely, stay in with your seatbelt on and call 999.

Can I travel in the truck with my car?

Yes — up to two passengers ride in the cab with the driver. If there are more of you, tell us when you call and we'll arrange a taxi.

Do you recover vans and motorbikes?

Anything up to 3.5 tonnes: cars, vans, 4x4s, campervans, motorbikes and quads. Bikes go on a dedicated cradle with soft straps.

How can I pay?

Card or contactless on the truck, cash, or bank transfer. Business accounts can be invoiced monthly.
