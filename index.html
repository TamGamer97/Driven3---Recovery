Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a member?

No. There’s no membership or subscription. Call us when you need us.

Is recovery free if the accident wasn’t my fault?

For eligible non-fault claims, recovery, storage and replacement vehicle costs may be recovered from the at-fault driver’s insurer, so you don’t pay upfront. It depends on liability and the details of the claim. Give us a call and we’ll go through it with you.

Where can you take my vehicle?

Home, your own garage, a dealer or our secure storage.

Can passengers travel with the vehicle?

Usually, yes. Let us know how many of you there are when you call.

Do you work outside Greater Manchester?

Yes. Most of our work is around Oldham, Rochdale and Manchester, but we recover and transport vehicles all over the UK.

Will you buy my old car?

If it’s scrap, yes, in any condition. If it’s in good condition, Driven3 Automotive may take it in part exchange.
